The BlackBull framework

Structure first.
Stories second.

Markets are uncertain. A repeatable study process helps separate observable price behaviour from assumptions and emotion.

01 / CONTEXT

Higher-timeframe structure

Study swing highs and lows, trend context and important price zones before focusing on lower-timeframe movement.

02 / PRICE ACTION

Liquidity & displacement

Learn how liquidity areas, impulsive moves and market-structure shifts are described—and where interpretations can fail.

03 / CONFIRMATION

Plan the invalidation

Define what would weaken an idea, what evidence is still missing and why no chart pattern guarantees an outcome.
