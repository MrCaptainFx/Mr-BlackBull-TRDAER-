Learning library

Build your market-reading skill

Short lessons with practical checkpoints.
01 · Structure

Market Structure & MSS

Learn the difference between a swing high/low, a break of structure and a meaningful shift. Ask what price has actually confirmed.

  • Mark obvious swing points
  • Wait for a candle close
  • Define what would invalidate the idea
02 · Liquidity

Liquidity without assumptions

Equal highs and lows can attract attention, but a level is not a guaranteed target. Learn to separate a sweep from a confirmed reversal.

  • Identify visible highs/lows
  • Note the reaction after a sweep
  • Avoid predicting the next candle
03 · Imbalance

FVG & Price Delivery

Understand a fair value gap as a price-imbalance concept. Not every gap is relevant; context and reaction matter more than the label.

  • Check displacement
  • Locate the imbalance
  • Record whether price reacts
